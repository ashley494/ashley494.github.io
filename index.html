AI operations system

Problem
Two businesses, three inboxes and a homeschool, all run by one person. Deadlines slipped and replies waited.
Fix
A set of Claude agents and always-on services, each with one job:
  • Inbox agents read all three inboxes and draft replies
  • A daily agent keeps the to-do list at three items and preps the next step
  • Check-ins twice a day and a Sunday review of money and drift
  • A Monday market brief with three decisions
  • A watchdog checks every job every 10 minutes and buzzes a phone once per problem
Result
20 systems running daily. Nothing goes out without one tap from a person.

Client project · Microsoft 365

Contractor timekeeping system

Problem
Contractor hours were kept in one person's personal OneDrive and compiled into the client's spreadsheet by hand.
Fix
Time-entry system in Microsoft 365 (SharePoint, Power Apps, Power Automate):
  • Each contractor can see and change only their own entries
  • Times convert to the right time zone, daylight saving included
  • Minutes round to the nearest 15
  • Every entry lands in the client's spreadsheet automatically, with a notification
  • Moved into the company's own account, with written instructions and access rules
Result
No more manual compiling. 3,740 contractor hours logged.

Content quality gate

LLM-as-judge evaluation
Draft→Pass or hold
Problem
AI drafts sounded like AI and used words owners don't know.
Fix
Every draft gets automatic checks, two separate AI readers (a 55-year-old business owner and a 12-year-old) and a 100-point score. Failing drafts are rewritten up to three times, and every rejection is logged with its reason.
Result
Only passing drafts reach a person.
